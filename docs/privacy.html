Privacy policy

Effective 22 September 2026 · Seller Australian Dynamics (Australia) · Bundle id com.josh12891.roofsetout

AU Roof Carpenter is published by Australian Dynamics (Australia). It is an on-site roof set-out calculator for Australian carpenters (gable ends, common rafter / birdsmouth, hip set-out, creeper schedule, and L/T junctions). It is offline-first, has no ads, and does not create accounts.

This page is the public policy for the App Store and Google Play. Plain language: we do not collect a profile of you, and we do not sell data.

What we collect

Essentially nothing beyond what Apple or Google already handle for a store purchase.

  • Measurements and calculations stay on your device. Lengths, pitches, bevels and set-out marks are not sent to our servers. We do not run an account database.
  • No login, analytics, advertising, or crash telemetry are bundled in this app.
  • Optional Pro unlock ($39.99 AUD, one-time) for hip / valley, creeper schedule (common difference, cutting list, material order) and L/T junctions (each includes one free calculation on this device first) is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They may keep purchase receipts under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your card number, and we do not get a copy of your full payment details. Product id: roof_setout_pro_unlock.
  • Small on-device flags may remember that you unlocked, and whether each paid tool’s free calculation was used, so the tools stay available offline. Those flags do not leave the device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not show ads or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not require an email address, name, or location permission to use the calculators.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. To review, restore, or refund a purchase, use your Apple ID or Google account settings, or those companies’ support. Their rules apply to receipts and payment data.

Australian users

The app is built for tradies in Australia. If we ever held personal information covered by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), we would handle it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Today the calculators themselves do not need that information to work.

Children

AU Roof Carpenter is a trade reference tool, not a children’s app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way (for example if a future build adds optional diagnostics), we will update this page before that release ships.

Questions about privacy: australiancomsnetwork@gmail.com.
